Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or use the GPS Converter (SVY21/MM2000) web application.

1. Personal Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data (such as names, emails, or phone numbers) directly. However, we use your device's Geolocation data strictly to perform coordinate conversions and surveying tasks within the app. This location data is processed locally on your device and is not sent to or stored on our servers.

2. Cookies and Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies, specifically Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

3. Offline Storage

To provide offline functionality (PWA), this app stores structural files (HTML, JS, CSS) and DXF map data locally in your browser's Cache and IndexedDB. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser history.
