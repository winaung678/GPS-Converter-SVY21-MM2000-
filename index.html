Overview of the 7 Main Modules

Our application is divided into seven dedicated modules to handle specific field tasks. Below is a detailed guide on how to use each feature effectively in your surveying workflow.

1. Global UTM (Universal Transverse Mercator)

This module allows you to convert standard WGS84 Latitude and Longitude (LL) into the global UTM projection system (Northing and Easting). It automatically detects your UTM Zone based on your longitude. You can input coordinates manually or turn on the GPS tracking mode to capture real-time topographic points directly into the built-in storage.

2. SVY21 Coordinate System (Singapore)

Designed specifically for projects in Singapore, this tool applies the localized Transverse Mercator projection to eliminate scale distortions. It provides seamless bidirectional conversion between WGS84 (LL) and local SVY21 (NE). Real-time MSL (Mean Sea Level) elevation is supported by utilizing the EGM2008 geoid model integration.

3. MM2000 Coordinate System (Myanmar)

For surveying tasks in Myanmar, the MM2000 datum is required. This module strictly handles the parameter shifts and projects WGS84 coordinates into the MM2000 local grid (typically Zone 46 or 47). It includes an offline database for EGM96 to calculate precise orthometric heights without internet access.

4. Set Out (Stakeout Navigation)

The Set Out module is your digital compass and distance guide. You can import CSV point files or select previously saved Topo points. Once a target point is selected, the application calculates the real-time distance and bearing from your current GPS location to the target, providing visual arrow directions and dynamic distance updates.

5. COGO (Coordinate Geometry Tools)

The COGO module is an advanced mathematical utility room containing three powerful sub-tools. (See the detailed COGO breakdown below).

6. Site Plan (DXF Viewer)

Surveyors no longer need heavy desktop software to view AutoCAD files in the field. This module uses an advanced Canvas Renderer to overlay 2D DXF site plans directly onto the map. You can toggle lines and texts, snap to nodes, and measure distances between DXF points with high accuracy. The map data is saved securely in your browser's IndexedDB for offline use.

7. Survey Camera

Documentation is critical. The Survey Camera module captures field photos and automatically stamps them with a real-time GPS watermark. The watermark includes the Project Name, Date, Local Datum (SVY21/MM2000/UTM), Northing, Easting, MSL Elevation, and Compass Heading, ensuring verifiable field evidence.

Deep Dive: The COGO Utilities

Within the COGO (Module 5) section, we provide three specialized calculation tools for on-the-fly problem-solving.

1. Area Calculator

Calculate the exact area of a land plot directly in the field. By selecting three or more target points from your uploaded CSV or recorded Topo list, the tool applies the mathematical Shoelace Formula. It instantly outputs the area in four distinct units: Square Meters (Sq.M), Square Feet (Sq.Ft), Acres, and Hectares. It also draws a visual polygon over the live map for verification.

2. Bearing & Distance (Inverse & Traverse)

This dual-function tool is the heart of traditional surveying:

  • Inverse Calculation: Enter the Northing and Easting of Point A and Point B. The app will calculate the exact straight-line Distance and the Polar Bearing (in both Decimal Degrees and DMS format) between the two points.
  • Traverse Calculation: If you know your starting coordinate (Point A), the distance you need to go, and the bearing angle, this tool will accurately predict the final coordinate (Point B).

3. Gradient / Elevation Calculator

Perfect for civil engineering, drainage, and road construction tasks. This tool manages slope percentages:

  • Calculate Gradient: Input your horizontal distance and the starting/ending elevations (Z1 and Z2) to find the exact slope percentage and 1:N ratio.
  • Predict Elevation (Z2): If you know your starting elevation (Z1), the distance, and the required design gradient (e.g., 2.5%), the app calculates the exact target elevation (Z2) you need to hit.
