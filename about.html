About Survey Pro

SVY21/MM2000 GPS CONVERTER is a professional surveying application designed for precision and reliability. Fully equipped with Coordinate Conversions, Stakeout capabilities, and Offline Maps.

Core Features

  • Datums: SVY21 & MM2000 (Auto Zone)
  • Global UTM: All Zones Supported
  • Set Out: Visual Guidance & Auto Zone
  • COGO Tools: Calculate Area from Points
  • Maps & DXF: Smooth Zoom & Auto Hide Text
  • Camera: Take Photos with GPS Watermark
