About Survey Pro

SVY21/MM2000 GPS CONVERTER is a professional surveying application designed for precision and reliability. Fully equipped with Coordinate Conversions, Stakeout capabilities, COGO utilities, and Offline Maps.

🌟 Core Features

🌍 Coordinate Datums: Seamless conversion between SVY21, MM2000 (Auto Zone), and Global WGS84 UTM.
🎯 Set Out Navigation: Visual guidance system with auto zone detection for precise point layout.
🧮 COGO Tools (Coordinate Geometry):
  • Area Calculator: Calculate land area (Sq.M, Acres, Hectares) from selected map points.
  • Bearing & Distance: Calculate grid inverse (Distance & Bearing) and traverse coordinates.
  • Gradient / Elevation: Calculate slope percentage and predict target elevation (Z2).
🗺️ Site Plan (DXF): Load and view AutoCAD DXF maps directly over the map with snap tools.
📷 Survey Camera: Take field photos with embedded GPS coordinates, Datum, and timestamp watermarks.
